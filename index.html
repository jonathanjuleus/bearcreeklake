See if you can get to the 11th hour: one 4.167-mile loop, every hour, for as long as you can, up to 11 laps before the park closes for the night. If more than one runner is still going into that final lap, whoever completes it fastest wins. It's one last race before winter takes over, run at the eleventh hour of the season. Starts 8:00 AM on Halloween.
