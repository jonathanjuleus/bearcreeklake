Meet your race directors

Race Director

Jon Juleus

Colorado trail runner, ultramarathoner, and the person who thought Bear Creek Lake Park needed its own backyard ultra.

Photo credit: Leah Williams, Jon's wife.

My First Ultra

This summer, Jon ran his first ultramarathon: Silverheels, a roughly 53.3-mile mountain race with about 9,500 feet of gain and a night start. He had so much fun out there, exhausted and all, that it got him thinking Colorado needed another race like it, closer to Denver, built around the same kind of scrappy, out-there spirit. Bear Creek Lake Park Backyard Ultra is the result.

Ephemeral Run Club

Jon also hosts a run club in Denver out of Ephemeral Brewing. If you're around Denver and want to run with the crew before race day, that's the place to find them.

Why this race exists

The backyard ultra format rewards patience and consistency over raw speed, and there wasn't one in this part of Colorado. Bear Creek Lake Park Backyard Ultra is Jon's answer to that: a low-key, well-run race close to Denver, built by someone who's stood at plenty of start lines himself.
